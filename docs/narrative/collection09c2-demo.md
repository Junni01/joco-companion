## 6. Rare intact disagreement: Madras → Hyderabad

Actual Shuffle, storm acknowledgement, phase finish, Parliament and New Turn resolvers leave three publicly known unordered Crisis events. Madras is exposed. This legal hypothetical conquest raises Company frontier-neighbour count from 2 to 3. Distinct cautious/competent adjutant and ambitious same-Presidency secretary are eligible.

**Thomas Hale** (army-madras; Madras Army adjutant):

> I advise restraint. A distant victory would leave us with one more frontier to defend.

**Henry Reed** (secretary-madras; Madras regional administrative adviser):

> And restraint would leave us with precisely the same frontiers to explain to our superiors.

Selected: `09C-exchange-prudence-vs-glory`.
