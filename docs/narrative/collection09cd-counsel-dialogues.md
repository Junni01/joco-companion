# Collection 09C-2 / 09D — Company advisers, action-dialog narrative and China outlook

**Status:** Approved **design direction**, not authorization to integrate new authored speech. Prepare a separate dialogue collection for human approval before coding. Read [09C-1 UI](collection09c-operations-ui.md) and [verified rules boundaries](collection09cd-rules-check.md).

## Approved player-experience contract — companion, not tutorial (25 October 2026)

**User's explicit product direction:** The intended player already has a basic grasp of John Company 2E actions and their rough procedures. This is **not a game-teaching tool**. Its first job is to make physical play easier without reaching for the rulebook for specific resolutions; its second job is to add compelling narrative atmosphere. Design and review all regular-mode UI against this principle.

- **Enforce the board-game rules wherever state is authoritative:** Make illegal actions and targets unavailable, validate required conditions and mandatory effects, apply known exceptions, and keep the physical board and companion state in sync. Prefer prevention to warnings when the app has enough data. Do not offer a mechanically invalid action merely to explain afterward why it fails. Never change the published rules under the guise of assistance.
- **Separate mechanical fidelity from narrative plausibility:** Actual rules-resolved outcomes and authoritative state changes remain exact. Player-reported inputs that the app cannot independently check (for example, committed force counts when physical Army inventory is untracked) must not be reclassified as independently verified facts; do not invent a second inventory system solely for narrative certainty. The resulting reported roll/outcome can still drive valid control and physical-board consequences. Narrative may supply colour, biased interpretations, boasting, anxiety, gossip and small fictional details without reproducing every mechanical count. Do not derive major casualties, deaths, promises, payments, trade receipts, or political transitions from unverified inputs. Keep the reliable chronicler distinct from fallible fictional voices; entertaining plausibility matters more than a literal mechanical transcript.
- **Honest enforcement boundaries:** The companion does not track every physical piece, officeholder decision, private holding or unreported action. Where legality depends on untracked physical facts, request only the minimal necessary information when essential, or give a concise physical verification instruction. Do not silently assume legality or claim complete enforcement. Rule enforcement must never introduce fake certainty or unwanted extra steps.
- **Assume action literacy:** Do not explain from scratch what Deploy, Trade, Administer, Special Envoy or India Phase means. Familiar, concise game terminology is fine.
- **Replace rulebook lookup, not the game:** When resolving an action, show the specific computed strength, legal choices, modifiers and exact physical instructions the player needs **for this actual board state**. Cover rare exceptions and cascading effects accurately; avoid generic definitions and tutorial paragraphs.
- **Show useful numbers, hide implementation:** Keep necessary mechanical information such as strength, Orders, unrest and required counters visible. Hide cache provenance, template IDs, guard predicates, state-machine commentary, save internals, diagnostic labels and developer explanations in normal play. Debug mode or a dedicated developer view may retain these temporarily.
- **Two-layer delivery:** Physical game results/instructions first; short, relevant historical-fiction reactions second. The story adds atmosphere but never prevents players from carrying out the physical instructions or forces additional confirmations.
- **Low friction:** Do not add read-acknowledgement checkboxes, proof-of-attention steps, redundant Next/Confirm stages, or extra reporting solely to establish narrative provenance. Rendering counsel in an actual pre-result UI stage is sufficient evidence the fictional adviser offered it; it does **not** prove the human read it or what motivated their choice.
- **No unsupported inferences:** Track actual render phase, committed decisions and outcomes. An adviser may say their recommendation wasn't followed when the relevant real choice was different, but cannot claim a player deliberately ignored advice through arrogance or that a failed check provided a post-success choice.
- **Future cleanup:** During/after 09D-2, audit every normal screen and action dialog. Classify texts as necessary action instructions, useful status, thematic fiction, redundant tutorial, or diagnostic scaffolding. Remove redundant explanations and relocate developer information to Debug without hiding actionable physical-piece requirements.
- **Review criterion:** A person who knows how to play the board game but not how the companion is implemented should understand what to do next immediately and without picking up the rulebook for the relevant resolution.

This clarifies product intent; do not refactor a Codex task already in progress or deploy Site solely for this documentation addition.

## 09D-2 mode-aware dialog composition — revised creative direction (25 October 2026)

**User decision:** Do not make narrative authors certify that dialogue repeats any predetermined outcome facts. The narrative must remain free-flowing, humorous, selective, contradictory and character-driven. An earlier proposal to suppress normal summaries according to fact-by-fact coverage metadata is **rejected**.

- **Both modes share a concise factual result title** describing the actual resolution (for example, "Bombay's Attack on Hyderabad Fails Catastrophically" or "Company Takes Control of Delhi"). That title is the main shared statement of what happened.
- **Both modes retain the exact actionable physical instructions, mandatory effects, essential numbers and legal decisions.** Where an event genuinely requires a separate result indication or a state-change detail to avoid ambiguity, keep it; do not force a character to narrate the rules.
- **Normal mode** uses the shared title and rules-resolution UI, with concise neutral supporting status only when genuinely useful.
- **Narrative mode** uses the same title and rules-resolution UI, plus chronicler/ruler/adviser scenes that may focus on irony, relationships, observations, unease, jokes or historical callbacks. No mandatory fact-repetition template slots, coverage matrices or dialogue fact checklists.
- Character selection still obeys **eligibility and provenance**: dialogue cannot invent consequential events, unreported officer deaths, received payments, actual completed China trade or other important unsupported factual history. Characters may boast, misunderstand, speculate and exaggerate as subjective speech.
- Avoid duplicating static summary paragraphs already supplied by the title. Don't force chronicler, ruler and adviser to recount the identical outcome. The chronicler may remain silent or spend their line entirely on comic interpretation.
- **User-selected 09D-2 presentation (25 October 2026):** Use a **separate narrative page within the existing action-resolution dialog**, **not** an inline narrative block on the physical-instructions page. The first page shows the factual title and actual physical instructions in both modes; Narrative mode then advances through the existing action progression to a dedicated story page. Normal mode finishes after instructions, without an empty story page or extra step. Preserve all actual protected resolution stages, Elephant instructions and event-stack recaps. Do not introduce new read acknowledgements or confirmation buttons. Navigation back to review instructions must not mutate committed state or regenerate speech.
- **Single-scene reading (user-approved):** The dedicated narrative page presents the entire selected conversation as **one scrollable scene**. Show multiple speakers' complete turns together with clear names and optional identity-stable portraits alongside them; keep the chronicler free to speak without a portrait. No line-by-line reveal, visual-novel click progression, mandatory 'continue' between utterances, or need to expand the main scene to read it. Use ordinary scrolling and the existing action dialog navigation. Secondary archived/history content may have its own expansion affordance, but never hide the active scene behind extra taps.
- **Future portraits:** Design the dedicated narrative page to accommodate optional portraits alongside free-form multi-speaker dialogue and longer scenes. Use persistent character identity for portraits when later available. **Do not** generate or invent production character portraits, add mandatory portrait slots, or block 09D-2 on Collection 10 identity/artwork.
- Scene placement is now directionally selected, but **09D-2 implementation is not yet authorized**; finalize specific UX flow and user approval first. Preserve accepted 09D-1 behavior.

## 09D-2 India Phase pacing — consequential and anticipatory scenes (user-approved, 25 October 2026)

**Critical distinction:** India Phase narrative alternates between **consequence** (a resolved actual Crisis/event and its fallout) and **anticipation** (Elephant movement plus a newly visible top-of-stack region). Do not suppress an anticipation scene merely because no crisis has yet resolved or because the transition has only minor immediate mechanical effects. This visible movement/reveal is a dramatic beat in itself.

- Preserve the existing game's true order and separate protected physical/instruction stages: resolve current event and its meaningful fallout; physically advance Elephant and event stack when the rules instruct; expose only the actually revealed public information; offer the anticipation scene; then resolve the next event. The narrative must follow the *actual* engine timing, not force an invented universal sequence for every event type.
- At the Elephant/top-of-stack beat, use **both distinct public facts** as available: Elephant's current region and the actual visible top-stack region, Company/independent control, unrest, recent regional changes, Army-related facts genuinely established, and prior history. They may be different regions; do not conflate them.
- High unrest in a Company-controlled region creates justified **alarm** and political speculation, not a guaranteed Rebellion or a prediction of a hidden event. A recent defeat can darken the outlook, while a quiet region may create complacency or unease. Some forecasts will prove wrong; that is part of the story.
- The top visible region is public location/context only. **Never use hidden card/tile face, unrevealed event order, future result or covert RNG** to select or phrase anticipation; preserve hidden-information protections and reproducible narration.
- After a substantial resolved event, give the result its own narrative scene where appropriate. Small sub-effects belonging to that same event can be included within that scene rather than generating extra pages. Yet Elephant movement and visible-stack transitions must remain narratively eligible between events—these are suspense beats, not redundant result summaries.
- Keep the user-approved dedicated **single scrollable narrative page** within existing action dialogs for scenes, with future optional portraits. Do not add per-line clicks, read acknowledgements, duplicate map toasts or extra invented gameplay decisions; preserve physical instructions and already-necessary transition acknowledgements.
- Scope control: this approves dramaturgical direction, **not** new anticipation prose nor 09D-2 implementation. Review actual current event-stack and Elephant state-machine ordering before a Codex handoff.

## 09D-2 India event narration policy — Option A accepted (25 October 2026)

**User approved Option A:** Every resolved India event type—including routine Turmoil, Windfall, Peace, Leader and Shuffle—gets an **opportunity** for its own narrative scene, even when the mechanical effect is small. A single chronicler remark can be sufficient; a major event can support a longer exchange. Do not gate scenes solely on the magnitude of mechanical change or arbitrarily silence a fixed percentage of routine events.

- **Creative freedom for small events:** The chronicler and characters may invent humorous *incidental* scenarios, small social embarrassments, misunderstandings, rituals, bureaucratic absurdities or local colour that plausibly accompany the actual event. Fiction is part of the entertainment, not a factual incident ledger. A quiet Windfall or routine Shuffle may warrant a great joke.
- **Incidental voices and Company social life (user-approved):** Routine events may feature **one-off, unnamed or role-identified fictional people**, including Company clerks/Writers, merchants, servants, accountants, ship captains, courtiers and townspeople. Their brief dialogue may provide humour and everyday life without creating persistent NPC records, offices, individualized game pieces, new transactions or historical commitments. In a Windfall, for instance, a fictional Company Writer may cheer about finally being paid; this is *flavour*, **not** a claim that any particular player Writer is on an Order or received the rule-defined £1. Company institutions can feel inhabited beyond the persistent named adviser cast. If an unnamed incidental voice later becomes narratively important, that requires a distinct consciously designed promotion into a persistent identity, not automatic proliferation of saved characters.
- **Consequences remain authoritative:** Do not assert unreported material payments to specific players, deaths, battles, troop losses, new alliances, real control changes or other game-changing facts. Distinguish subjective claims and incidental fictional embellishment from actual tracked outcomes. The mechanical result and physical instructions are always exact.
- **Flexible scene size and casting:** One chronicler line is enough; sometimes a ruler or adviser joins, and multi-region Cascade effects may produce multiple voices in one scene. No enforced character count, factual-coverage checklist, obligatory multi-line quota or maximum dialogue length. Avoid generic filler if no suitable approved writing is available.
- **Group subeffects, not distinct suspense beats:** An individual Order closing, Writer returned, tower adjustment or unrest removal should normally be part of its parent event's scene rather than a new page. The distinct Elephant-movement and newly public top-of-event-stack suspense scene remains eligible between events even if no crisis has yet occurred.
- **No implementation authorization:** This is approved narrative direction for future 09D-2 writing and UI design; new exact production dialogue requires its own creative review. Keep accepted 09D-1 and current mechanics intact.

## Future Parliament phase opening scene — proposed direction (25 October 2026)

**User idea under creative review, not implementation authorization:** After Events in India and as Parliament Meets begins, offer a humorous theatrical scene in Westminster featuring **fictional, incidental MPs** voicing grievances about the East India Company before the vote. This broadens the story from India to political life in Britain; MPs need not be persistent NPCs or players' own officeholders.

Historical context: parliamentary criticism of Company patronage, military policy, corruption, nabobs, territorial sovereignty and profits intensified especially after the 1760s; extensive investigations and debates took place in the 1770s and 1780s. The game's phase structure provides artistic license to revisit such controversies in every turn, but avoid inventing verbatim historical speeches or falsely assigning fictional lines to real MPs.

**Timing suggestion for review:** According to the game rulebook Parliament Meets (p.34), the Prime Minister first draws/selects a law, selects a policy, reads the law and establishes its starting support, then members debate before voting. If the scene should comment on the selected law or policy, place it in the **existing pre-vote debate window**, after the law/policy is known and before voting starts. An atmospheric arrival-in-Parliament scene on phase entry is an alternative, but must not pretend to know the yet-unselected law. Do not move the real game steps or add a fake vote, policy choice or read-acknowledgement.

Creative eligibility: parliamentary speeches can comment on the **publicly recorded preceding India Phase**, cumulative disasters/successes, trade disruptions, known political shifts and the actually selected law when entered; no assumption about untracked dividends, specific families' payments, actual votes or hidden information. Opposed MPs may contradict one another for humour; self-serving hypocrisy is encouraged, but it's not evidence of a gameplay bribe or patronage deal. Occasional one-speaker speeches or short parliamentary exchanges should be adequate; separate narrative scene, no line-by-line click-through. Treat this as a proposed extension beyond 09D-2's authorized scope until user settles timing and reviews prose.

## Revenue UX — user-proposed light reporting for shareholder narrative (25 October 2026)

**User proposal, in design review (not yet Codex implementation authorization):** Revenue works as concise physical board instructions in **Normal mode**, with **no fictional shareholder scene or extra financial reporting**. In **Narrative mode**, after the same real Revenue steps, ask only a few quick touch-friendly questions to supply a comic or serious meeting of Company stockholders/proprietors: **dividends paid, emergency loans taken, and current Company Standing**. The shareholders have family wealth exposed to Company performance. Scene uses the approved single scrollable dedicated narrative page with short exchanges/possible incidental investors; no fake stockholder vote or financial mechanics.

**Rules-verified refinement (Revenue, printed p.27):** Dividends can be paid **multiple times**, so capture a count (0,1,2,...), not only yes/no. Emergency loans can likewise be multiple; 1–2 and 3+ carry different Public Embarrassment adjustments, so a count preserves useful tone and factual distinction. Standing is a physical track position, preferably capture the actual position (or faithfully mapped category if only narrative classification is needed); do not estimate based on dividend/loan count alone, because Expectations and Public Enthusiasm and other gameplay effects matter. Physical game proceeds: Expenses and emergency loans, Check Expectations, Pay Dividends, resulting Standing. If Standing reaches the leftmost space, Company fails and **goes immediately to Final Scoring**, not onward to Events in India/Parliament.

UI guidance: plus/minus controls, no manual typing or player-family/share inventories. Don't ask to enter balance, debt ledger, share ownership, or payments to individual players. Treat results as player-reported context for scene eligibility; don't claim independently audited shareholder returns. Normal mode only needs accurate Revenue instructions; no superfluous reporting. Narrative mode results are used only for narrative selection unless a separately approved real rules/state enforcement integration is added. Avoid replaying a prior scene on Back/Undo without committed-state integrity.

Creative possibilities: no dividends triggers dismay, high dividends trigger self-serving praise, emergency loans provoke panic or rationalization, Standing colours confidence. Incidental shareholders can argue and contradict one another. Fictional investment worries are welcome; don't invent a specific player's losses or unreported Company finance facts. No new rule choices or extra acknowledgements.

## Revenue failure as shareholder epilogue — user-approved direction (25 October 2026)

**User decision:** Since Company failure may occur during Revenue, the Revenue flow must support an **end-game branch**. When the Company fails, replace the ordinary shareholder scene with a **dedicated final shareholders' epilogue** in Narrative mode, then guide players directly to published Final Scoring. In Normal mode, provide the actual final scoring instructions with no fictional page. Do not continue into Events in India or Parliament after Revenue failure.

**Rules:** Revenue printed p.27: if Standing reaches the leftmost track space, the Company fails and proceeds to Final Scoring **immediately**. This may occur from emergency loans/Public Embarrassment or failed Expectations before the Chairman would pay dividends; do not require completing hypothetical later Revenue steps. Other failure points have their own distinct timing: failure during Events in India finishes that phase before Final Scoring (p.31), and a legislated Company failure finishes the vote (p.34). Never repurpose the Revenue timing for all endings.

**UI:** Offer a clear narrative-aware end-game path and preferably derive failure from the entered actual Standing position, avoiding a redundant 'failed?' confirmation. A user may need to report that failure occurred at an earlier Revenue step rather than supplying nonexistent completed-dividend information. Respect back navigation, save/undo and existing physical stage semantics. Don't introduce a second Company-failure vote, payout, or homebrew rule.

**Creative:** The last shareholders' gathering can be longer and more theatrical, with incidental fictional proprietors, secretaries and chronicler, contrasting their financial anxieties with the Company's actually recorded history. It may refer to known territory, disasters and Company reputation, but not invent specific player-family fortune losses or financial details not reported. Creative humour or sober endings are both permissible. Store an actual end-state distinction so the ordinary scene is not also shown for the same Revenue outcome.

## Check-first action choice and narrative consequences

Special Envoy and Governor Administer grant a **choice of effect after successful checks** under the actual game rules. The companion may collect an intended preference **before** the player reports the physical check to support better narrative, but this cannot lock a post-success choice. On a successful check, permit lawful revision/choice of effect and distinguish pre-check recommendation from post-check commentary. On failure, no successful effect choice exists, so an adviser cannot claim their recommendation was deliberately rejected.

Governor Administer remains a **regional Governor action**. A continuing regional assistant may recommend an eligible Tax, Commission Regiment, or Build Company Ship as a provisional plan; after success, respond to the actual selected effect. The Governor General law replaces offices, so any comparable commentary must use its valid powers and adviser rather than pretend regional Governors remain.

Avoid assuming a failed Envoy check was caused by a hostile court; a competent clerk may privately spin the failure but the chronicler does not certify rejected negotiations.

**Advice preview is ephemeral**: re-evaluate a preview only when Army/target/major plan or relevant public facts materially change. Do not rotate on every +/- click, map hover or panel read. Opening, changing target and cancelling must not append a saved incident, mutate personality or consume narrative/game RNG.

**Commit atomicity**: When a real attempt is committed, save exactly what recommendation was actually visible at commitment, adviser identity, action intent/final selected effects, public evidence, and player-reported outcome only if known. A changed target/effect invalidates the prior preview, requiring accurate replacement counsel or explicitly no counsel. Never credit the adviser with statements displayed only in cancelled planning. If the app has a multi-stage durable attempt, preserve the single transaction boundary and reload-safe draft without creating historical speech before commitment.

**Action dialog placement (09D)**: Show gameplay result and physical instructions clearly first, then relevant chronicler/Indian ruler/Company adviser reactions in the still-visible resolution dialog; preserve independent Elephant instruction page and exposed stack recap. Avoid toast-only important speech. Chronicle continues to store exact utterances and supported provenance. Don't duplicate all speech on map.

**Callbacks**: Later remarks may be based on a previously **committed** saved recommendation and later real consequences. A real failure doesn't prove a prudent strategy would have succeeded.

## 09D adviser rivalries — approved design direction, implementation deferred

**User decision, 25 October 2026:** Company advisers **may develop rare, emergent rivalries** from meaningful, **recorded** disagreements and the subsequent actual outcomes. Do **not** assign automatic enmities by institution (e.g., Army versus China), personality archetype, or random setup. Institutionally different priorities can produce a disagreement without necessarily producing hostility.

- **09D-1 — historical evidence:** Save the precise *actually displayed and committed* counsel, speaker IDs, action/effect, stance, whether the official followed it when a genuine post-success choice was available, and the real outcome. Do not save abandoned dialog previews as an incident. A failed Special Envoy or Administer check does **not** imply the official disregarded advice, because no successful choice arose.
- **09D-2 — scene delivery:** Physical instructions and authoritative results stay first. Present relevant in-universe reactions inside the actual resolution dialog; preserve separate Elephant instruction pages and event-stack recap, with scene text retained in chronicle.
- **09D-3 — rare relationship evolution:** Repeated substantial, **publicly or jointly witnessed** disagreements and later actual outcomes may change fictional adviser-to-adviser respect, irritation, rivalry or reluctant admiration. A single contrary recommendation normally means nothing enduring. Avoid automatically making everyone rivals. Prefer an explicit, bounded relationship incident derived from existing structured action/statement IDs rather than an arbitrary hostility counter.
- **Knowledge boundaries:** A private ruler outlook, silent adviser's thought, canceled planning preview, unspoken preference or unreported player motive is **not** knowledge another adviser may quote. Only genuinely spoken/displayed counsel that the relevant character could plausibly have heard, public outcomes or established institutional records support cross-speaker callbacks. Do not treat a saved private note as a public Company memorandum by default.
- **Narrative disagreement:** The Army adviser may argue for defending exposed territory; the China adviser may value newly accessible **verified printed opium icons**. Each view can be self-serving without mechanically modifying choices. Future callbacks may involve embarrassment, self-justification, respectful concession or occasional collaboration, not only escalation.
- **Continuity:** Persist relationship provenance, involved stable character IDs, original exact statement refs and noteworthy outcomes. Changing physical player officeholders doesn't erase fictional NPC relationships. Replacements inherit institutional history, not a predecessor's personal grudge. Respect death, exile, dismissal and eligibility.
- **Pacing:** Cross-institution dialogue and rivalry callbacks are exceptional moments, not obligatory commentary on every deployment; no grinding relationship changes by opening/closing dialogs. Resolve specific source predicates and approved authored prose before implementation. No inventing meetings, personal insults, promises, battles, trade receipts, payments or causal claims.
- **Scope:** This approves the *design*, not authoring or shipping 09D. Finish the already-scoped 09C-2 adviser previews first; then implement 09D incrementally (committed advice, placement, callbacks/rivalries) after separately approved exact dialogue and engineering review.
