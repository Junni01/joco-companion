# Persistent Character Engine — staged Codex handoff

**26 October 2026 · Design/implementation plan.** Approved concepts: automatic succession; distinct confirmed-dead/exiled/presumed-dead states; exiles relatively likely to return when eligible, genuine presumed-dead survivors exceptionally rarely; recurring impostors with independent identities; Peace may rarely marry two adjacent houses; game mechanics remain authoritative. **Only Milestone 1 below is proposed for immediate Codex implementation.** Later milestones require separately approved specifications and writing.

## Observed repository baseline

- `dist/narrative.js` currently creates eight ruler + younger-sibling pairs, six Presidency advisers and one general secretary: **23 characters**, each with stable `id`, `name`, `region`, `age`, `dynasty`, `role`, `condition`, `position`, `family`, `claims`, `relations`, `incidents`, `background`.
- The active seat map `n.seats[region]` points to `ruler-region`. Current role validator accepts ruler/heir/secretary/army; life conditions accept alive/dead/missing/imprisoned. `position` follows map control as a string, but is not an independent canonical office/claim record.
- `commitNarrative` is the committed-event boundary. It is where character relationships and map reactions get history/evidence. `next-turn` currently ages all not-confirmed-dead characters by **three years**, once per turn. Do not accidentally change this in foundation work.
- Current narrative version validator expects version **2**. Existing 09E automatic staged dialogue and save/Undo/Redo/protected invasion flow are the baseline. Do not regress them.
- The established narrative RNG is separate from gameplay RNG. Narrative presentation and roster read must not consume either RNG or mutate history.
- Region changes come from `dist/actions.js` and its incident facts. Do not infer a character casualty from reported officer routs, failed rebellions, unrest, invasions or other abstractions.

## Milestone 1: stable identities, fate and role foundation (next Codex task)

**Scope:** Extend existing 23-character schema and UI in a minimal backwards-compatible fashion. This is NOT yet a generator for marriage, mortality, replacement, rebellion leaders, imprisonments, exile, presumed death or impostors. It must establish well-defined durable representation and validators so later story systems can use them. No new fictional production dialogue.

### Schema / migration contract to implement

- Keep existing character IDs, names, portraits/silhouettes, family links, allegiance data, traits, relations, ages, incidents and preexisting saved quoted text **unchanged**.
- Choose the least disruptive optional current-schema extension (or explicitly justified version bump + migration). Do not silently reject current-version saved games because newly optional fields are absent.
- Add a canonical **life/fate** representation distinguishing `alive`, `dead` (confirmed, permanently nonreturnable), `exiled` (known survivor outside power), `presumed-dead` (unconfirmed fate; explicitly not `dead`), existing `missing` and `imprisoned`. Prefer retaining the current `condition` field to minimize callers; document its semantics. No hidden `secretlyAlive` flag for presumed dead.
- Add separate **political status / role history** as an extensible optional narrative-only representation (so `role:'ruler'` vs seat assignment are not conflated with exile, regency, vassalage or a claimant). Preserve existing `role` reader compatibility; do not retroactively change whose map region is controlled by whom.
- Store status/role transitions with provenance: origin incident/action + turn, previous/next status, and any reason that can be stated truthfully. Existing characters with no transitions must not be assigned invented fates.
- Make clear that an eligible claimant can remain `exiled` or `presumed-dead` without being a current ruler. **All actual roster seats must still point at existing valid living/eligible characters; don't replace a ruler just because a fate label exists.**
- Validate enums, references, bounded incident history, and impossible contradictions: a confirmed dead person cannot be installed as current ruler or transition to alive; status changes cannot happen during render; duplicate identity IDs are invalid. Do not apply future spouse/child constraints until those schemas are introduced.
- On old current-schema saves, default absent fields safely by interpretation; avoid backfilling fictional episodes or changing historical events. If migration is necessary, explicitly document and test it.
- Preserve current simple sibling relationship; no forced relabelling as child/heir and no fresh autogenerated dynasty members.

### UX scope

- Keep existing roster and dossier; show clear `Alive`, `Exiled`, `Presumed dead`, `Confirmed dead`, `Missing`, `Imprisoned` labels and known role/status history where actually recorded.
- An unmodified existing campaign should look as before. No new player data entry, fate controls, succession dialogs, Debug-only fate editor or management minigame in production.
- Never reveal a presumed-dead character's future return or a secret survival decision via roster, tooltip, export, summary or archive. No predetermined survival boolean.
- Retain existing scene readability and all 09E automatically presented scenes; do not reintroduce Read scene buttons.

### Tests / acceptance

1. Verify existing fresh-game cast remains exactly **23**; the eight region seats still point to their same actors, with no new names or family inventions.
2. Legacy current-version narrative loads, and save/import/export, snapshot, Undo/Redo, regular/debug separation and pending protected gameplay all behave unchanged.
3. Unit-test each fate label with constructed **test fixtures only**, including exiled/presumed-dead contrast; no actual gameplay creates these statuses yet.
4. Reject invalid references, contradictory status transitions and a confirmed-dead seat; preserve stable IDs and saved exact dialogue across restoration of backups.
5. Confirm ageing +3 on New Turn once, and current narrative version/migration behavior. Treat age interpretation as a later design choice, not a changed mechanic.
6. All existing mechanical parity, 09E weather/invasion and character-cast regressions remain green. Test desktop/tablet in both regular/Debug, Normal/Narrative including quiet.
7. A documented before/after demo shows the unchanged cast and test-only fate/dossier rendering, with no invented narrative incidents or changes to board state.

**Delivery:** Work in `dist/` and tests; push implementation/docs to `main`, report commit hashes, test counts, migration choices, demonstration and any incompatibilities. No Site deployment.

## Milestone 2: families and automatic succession (later separate approval)

Enhance family records with explicit spouse/parent/child, persistent birth/introduction chronology, eligibility, regional claim provenance, widow-regency and scenario-specific cultural constraints. Do not turn the current generic younger-sibling example into a son or daughter automatically. Automatic succession is triggered by a committed, narratively justified ruler exit; a replacement must preserve actual region sovereignty/control. No extra player succession choice. Define approximate ageing and rare mortality only after evaluating scenario-turn timelines and suitable storytelling cadence.

## Milestone 3: Peace and Leader (later separate approval)

Border Peace: regard/trust/grievance changes; rare marriage only between participating neighbouring houses with eligible adults; no game modifier. Internal Peace: domestic stability and subtle Company attitude. Sovereign Leader: preserve actual strength +1, usually consolidation, occasionally heirs/ministers, very rarely a coherent character succession. Non-sovereign Leader: wait for actual rebellion outcome before attributing success. Every narrative transition persists and is provenance-linked.

## Milestone 4: exile/restoration and impostors (later separate approval)

Reuse established exiled claimants most often when an actual compatible political change/rebellion occurs. Presumed-dead characters may rarely emerge as genuine survivors, with no hidden predetermined status and no victory guarantee. Confirmed dead never return. Impostors receive separate immutable IDs and may recur after failure; cannot replace the genuine claimant's identity. No companion-managed rebellion probability or claim-driven conquest effects. Review actual reveal timing, named VIP eligibility, selection weights and dialogue before implementation.

## Out-of-scope for Milestone 1

- No automatic death, exile, presumed-death, imprisonment, recovery, return, marriages or generation of new NPCs.
- No changes to John Company 2E outcomes, event counts, phase flow, legality, dice or physical input.
- No added prose, new character portraits, phase coordinator, Revenue/Parliament, deck/outlook probabilities or historical-retcon migration.
- No hosted deployment.

## Editorial invariants

Historical-world glossary applies. 'Presumed dead' is a public uncertainty, not confirmed death. A character may be absent from power while still significant to the story. All major consequential incidents must be tied to real committed events; no player decisions about successors, false reports about deaths, or undisclosed family claims.
