# Collection 09A/09B — implementation and engineering review

Implemented 25 October 2026 against the approved specification and decisions. **09C/09D are not implemented.** No hosted Site deployment is part of this delivery. New catalogue prose is bundled for creative review, not declared creatively approved.

## Architecture and decisions

`narrative-outlooks.js` contains a public projection boundary, pure regional assessment, deterministic authored selection and transactional updates. Its projection contains public region conditions, Elephant position, the visible next back's region, publicly revealed event type, possible remaining type counts, actual allegiance origins and an optional known returned batch. It never exports tile IDs, undrawn fronts/order, saved Shuffle permutations, unreported Army inventories or player motives. Forecast selection receives this projection, not the deck. Personal interpretation separately reads only that ruler's traits, confidence, owned memories and sourced relationship causes.

The public batch lives under optional narrative-only `foresight`: unordered event-type counts, remaining draw count and the actual Shuffle incident ID. Formation uses **public discards before the real Shuffle resolves**. A reveal consumes one known member; resolution does not consume it twice. Exhaustion clears it. A subsequent real Shuffle replaces the batch; manual test events do not change it. Debug setup corrections conservatively clear batch knowledge, including board-only corrections, because debug may alter the disclosure boundary. Undo/Redo restores the exact prior knowledge instead of recalculating from hidden positions.

Verified the current resolver against page 31 of the rulebook linked from the [publisher's resources](https://wehrlegig.com/pages/resources): normal March precedes the two separate shuffles and returned discards go above the unseen remainder; an empty remaining pile rebuilds before March. Neither rule nor resolver changed. The empty-pile case intentionally withholds the rebuilt top back until the real resolution exposes it.

Regional assessments distinguish sovereign frontier expansion/defence, weaker and stronger visible Indian positions, a subject's rebellion against its capital alone, capital anxiety, Company primary exposure and other Company unrest as **potential secondary** exposure. Company territory's zero tower is never treated as an empty Army: its untracked defence remains unknown. A known all-Crisis returned batch supports a strong military climate; a mixed Crisis/Peace batch supports uncertainty and accommodation. Normal counts and an exposed back never establish a secret front or a guaranteed target/outcome.

Exact current thoughts live in `narrative.outlooks[region]`: private visibility, original character ID, template and text, source incident, bounded public context, selected personal interpretation, owned memory IDs and relationship reason IDs. No thoughts are appended to statements, dialogue, promises or other characters' memories. Existing mood, relationships, journal scenes, chapters and narrative RNG are unchanged. Import validation checks the saved catalogue wording, private identity, structured context shape, source/owned evidence references, batch bounds and eligible stored selection. History deletion guards include these dependencies.

One independent, stable narrative-only aptitude per ruler is saved in `foresight.aptitudes`. It is derived from the initial narrative seed/state and character ID without consuming RNG; it is not inferred from caution or ambition. A confident low-aptitude character can underestimate otherwise eligible danger. No aptitude development, adviser competence system or new permanent relationship dimension was added. This is a modest interpretation mechanism, not an optimal strategy solver.

Reading, selection, profiles, rendering and export perform no mutation or sampling. Material committed public/personal changes can reconsider an outlook. Equal selected template and context retain the saved object; routine unchanged situations retain the words. Public context can update while the same thought legitimately survives. There is no automatic quip rotation or mandatory conversation.

Older **current-schema** saves may omit the new fields: they show no saved thought until a commit creates one. Missing disclosure history means **no known returned batch**, never reconstruction from hidden order. This is conservative feature initialization, not an old-save migration or rewriting of existing dialogue. Existing current-schema autosave, snapshots, JSON import/export, protected reveal state and Undo/Redo are retained.

## Small creative-review handoff and limitations

[Readable demonstration](collection09-demo.md) is deliberately small: contrasting frontier rulers, two temperament interpretations of identical public circumstances, a real threatening Shuffle, unchanged repeated reads, a resolved event with a legitimately unchanged thought and the outlook after known-batch exhaustion. Its synthetic starting discard/deck setup is explicitly disclosed. Regenerate with `node scripts/export-outlook-demo.mjs`. Browser/test summaries are in `collection09-verification.json`.

The catalogue has 22 short new private assessments. They express provisional judgment rather than definite mobilisation, negotiation, payment, loot transfer, betrayal or battle outcomes. I reviewed them for component language and certainty; automated terminology checks also pass. Contrasts are intentionally restrained. Whether these thoughts sound sufficiently individual and whether repeated watchfulness becomes monotonous remain user/ChatGPT creative-review questions.

This representative slice is not exhaustive event-specific forecasting: dedicated court-intrigue and foreign-rumour prose, sophisticated probabilistic reasoning, prediction arcs and later revisiting private beliefs remain deferred. No thought supplies public NPC knowledge. Pre-action Company counsel, conflicting advisers, speech relocation and post-action advice callbacks are 09C/09D and absent. No portraits, succession, inventories, finances, runtime AI, unrelated refactoring or Site deployment were added.
