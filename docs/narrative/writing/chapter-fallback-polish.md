# Collection 08B — Fallback Voice and Closing Coda

**Status:** User-approved proposed wording, 25 October 2026.  
**Scope:** Creative polish for Collection 08B thematic chapters; this document does **not** itself authorise application code changes.  
**Companions:** [Thematic chapter contract](thematic-chapters.md), [Commerce and chronicles](commerce-and-chronicles.md), [Style guide](style-guide.md), [08B implementation review](../collection08b-implementation.md).

The first 08B review found that the evidence guards were appropriately conservative, but the neutral fallbacks sounded like test reports: “turn 1,” “Tax demand,” and “available at the close” were prominent in the displayed prose. The passages below supply complete authored replacements without broadening eligibility. They should not be assembled from arbitrary sentence fragments. Their facts and identity parameters must still be verified against committed incidents and the saved chapter boundary. The final coda is an **additional conditional thread**, not a universal game-end speech.

## CH08-FACT-ADMIN-CONTINUED — Earlier conquest, later failed administration

> The Company had already taken {region}. It could still demand revenue there; an attempt to administer the place was less successful. Its hold on the region survived the effort.

**Guard:** Conquest predates the chapter's turn; a Tax demand and genuine unsuccessful administration occur in the current turn, in that order; the Company still controls the region at the chapter boundary. “Demand revenue” does not claim anyone paid. This passage does not attribute a personal opinion to the former ruler.

## CH08-FACT-TRADE-OPEN — Disruption followed by restoration

> A trading opportunity in {region} was interrupted, then restored. The Company had regained an opening. What might pass through it remained a separate question.

**Guard:** The same recorded opportunity is disrupted and restored during the current turn and remains available at the chapter boundary. This does not claim a sale, profit or reopening of every market. If a later recorded closure reverses it, use the reclosed variant instead.

## CH08-FACT-TRADE-CONTINUED — Restoration of an earlier disruption

> The earlier interruption of a trading opportunity in {region} was reversed. The opening remained available; the Company would have to make its own use of it.

**Guard:** The interruption belongs to an earlier turn; restoration occurs in the current turn; the same opportunity remains available at the chapter boundary. “Earlier” must not hide a later closure or imply newly completed commerce. The future-looking second clause is not a claim about an actual subsequent trade.

## CH08-FACT-RESTORED — Restoration without a same-turn disruption

> A trading opportunity in {region} became available once more. The Company had an opening; the outcome of any venture through it was another matter.

**Guard:** A recorded restoration occurs in the current turn and the opportunity remains available at the chapter boundary. Do not require or invent a same-turn disruption, completed sale, revenue or particular merchant.

## CH08-FACT-TRADE-RECLOSED — Restoration reversed before the chapter

> The Company regained a commercial opening in {region}, only to see it disrupted again. The recovery was real; its duration less impressive.

**Guard:** The same opportunity is restored and then closed again before the chapter boundary. It is closed at that boundary. The second sentence is the chronicler's dry assessment of the recorded sequence, not a merchant's saved opinion.

## CH08-FACT-LOSS — Loss of Company control

> The Company lost {region} and had not recovered it by the close. The account of its authority there could no longer be written in the present tense.

**Guard:** The Company loses control during this turn and remains out of control at the chapter boundary. No ruler fate, military withdrawal, personal escape or independence beyond the actual political state is implied.

## CH08-FINAL-GRIEVANCE — Unresolved conquest grievance at campaign end

> The campaign ended with the Company still holding {region}. {person}’s grievance had outlived the conquest that made it. The chronicler could record possession. Agreement was another matter.

**Guard:** This is a possible **final coda** only. A Company conquest occurred in an earlier turn; the same named character held the relevant seat at the loss and still has a sourced unresolved conquest grievance; the Company controls that region at game end. Verify the current personal interpretation or relationship reason does not show that the grievance has been resolved. Do not use merely because a conquest happened, and do not reassign the grievance to a successor. The coda adds no promise of later recovery or reconciliation. Do not show it alongside an ordinary final-turn chapter if that would exceed the intended pacing budget or repeat the same thought.
