## Boundary case: not speaking is correct

If no verifiably pre-action visible speech exists, or source/stance does not match actual attempted action, use a **neutral existing event reaction** or silence. Do not invent a warning to enable a callback. Failure alone is never proof an adviser was ignored.

## Approved editorial decisions and memory guardrails

- Preserve each exact authored line and ID. Existing approved `09D-*` lines in the 09C catalogue remain unchanged; select only compatible, evidence-supported responses.
- A recommendation can **encourage** an expedition while **acknowledging danger**. Supporting the undertaking does not imply promising success, predicting a precise outcome or endorsing all of the eventual physical choices.
- Track a factual distinction between **stance**, **stated reservation/qualification**, **expressed confidence**, **actual outcome**, and the speaker's later **subjective interpretation**. Derive this from metadata of approved lines and the committed action, not hindsight guesses.
- Record both actually spoken sides of an eligible two-adviser exchange if the associated attempt commits, even if neither later reacts. Cancelled planning creates no public historical disagreement.
- No simplistic "correct forecast" counter. Later selective self-justification is possible, but the exact utterances and real results remain reliable.
- Catastrophic-failure responses should sometimes be sober and should never make tragedy or unreported deaths a joke. Routine events may receive no adviser response.
