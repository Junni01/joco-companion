# Collection 09 — Outlooks and Counsel writing brief

**Status:** User-approved writing principles and **illustrative examples**, **not automatically approved production dialogue**. Review new lines before claiming creative approval. In-universe dramatic prose never announces game mechanics.

## Non-negotiable separation

- **UI mechanics/instructions:** may explicitly say Elephant, event, Crisis, Leader, Shuffle, discard pile, unrest cubes, dice, numbers and strength because players need accurate rules information.
- **Chronicler and fictional characters:** speak as eighteenth-/early nineteenth-century participants experiencing politics, commerce, military danger, negotiations, court rumour, loyalty and discontent. They do **not** refer to tiles, decks, discards, dice, probabilities, turns, cubes, Elephant pieces, numbered crises, mechanical bonuses or guaranteed forthcoming drawn events.
- **Private thoughts:** stand for the character's personal outlook; do not count as a public speech, promise or another character's knowledge. Render as internal reflection or assessment. They can be wrong.
- **Forecast knowledge:** translate verified public clues into *rumour/anticipation*, not invented definite off-screen army mobilization, sabotage, payments, conspiracy or diplomacy. An actual post-resolution Peace can be *fictionally interpreted* as successful bargaining, but it cannot invent a real treasure payment or treaty.
- **Character voice:** dry, restrained, historically plausible humour; serious about human tragedy. Faction/nationality does not dictate personality, intelligence or loyalty. Competence differs from caution, cowardice, ambition or greed.
- **Pacing:** one short thought per portrait; change only after committed meaningful developments, not selection/reload. For future 09C, usually one adviser and sometimes two genuinely conflicting advisers. Make silence possible.

## Situations worth writing

| Verified mechanical context (authoring metadata only) | In-world concerns |
| --- | --- |
| Empire-capital Elephant pointing toward an independent region | Imperial ambition, defensive vigilance, exposed frontier |
| Strong or weak sovereign facing rival | Confidence, bluff, caution or fear |
| Subject region under possible rebellion | Long-held resentment, fear of failed independence, imperial anxiety |
| Elephant inside Company region with real unrest | Vulnerable occupation, anticipation of rebellion, brittle assurances |
| Secondary Company unrest exposure | Wider instability beyond the obvious threatened province |
| Known dangerous top batch after Shuffle, including three Crisis fronts | Political climate deteriorating, growing alarm or opportunism—not a known number of events |
| Mixed Crisis/Peace returning | Fear of conflict tempered by hope of negotiation |
| Leader/Turmoil/Peace/Windfall possible | Court uncertainty, commerce threatened or restored, genuine diplomatic possibilities |
| Event top-back region visible | Regional attention without claiming to know hidden front |
| Persistent grievance amid successful interventions | Contradictory gratitude and resentment |
| Warning ignored in a future Deploy | Understated exasperation, overconfidence, opportunism, limited post-fact hindsight |

## Illustrative style samples — not integrated approval

**Imperial sovereign, ambitious, on an exposed frontier:**  
> "The lesser courts grow nervous. I have always found uncertainty an excellent time to remind one's neighbours of their obligations."

**Defending sovereign:**  
> "There is talk of war in every court. No one seems certain who will begin it, which makes me suspect everyone."

**Company secretary when a dangerous military climate is publicly apparent:**  
> "Reports from across India grow less reassuring by the day. I advise reinforcing our position rather than acquiring another province to defend."

**Glory-seeking Army adjutant, contradicting the secretary:**  
> "A most sensible recommendation. Unfortunately, the province we propose to acquire possesses a considerable treasury."

**Secretary after a lost expedition they opposed:**  
> "I would derive greater satisfaction from having been correct if the consequences were not quite so extensive."

**Company-controlled ruler with unrest:**  
> "The Company calls this unrest a temporary inconvenience. I wonder how many temporary inconveniences it can endure."

**Ruler privately conflicted after real beneficial interventions:**  
> "Their soldiers have saved my position more than once. I find myself grateful, which is an even greater irritation."

**Threatened frontier ruler expecting negotiations:**  
> "An army at one's frontier makes negotiations remarkably urgent. I hope the Emperor has come to discuss friendship rather than its price."

These are voice references; re-evaluate facts and character eligibility before any verbatim incorporation. The current 09A/09B coding authorization does not approve all this prose and does not authorize 09C/09D implementation.

## Dialogue quality review

Test for: mechanical leakage; certainty where there is only suspicion; alleged transactions not tracked; different interpretations of identical public conditions; consistent memories and personality; deliberate restraint; callbacks to *actually* saved private beliefs vs actually spoken words; no retrospective advice selection based on later outcomes. Human review determines final quality, not test counts alone.
