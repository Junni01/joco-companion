## Selection acceptance for Codex

- Choose the **locally/personally most relevant eligible concern** first, then choose a personality-sensitive expression. A broad dangerous climate should not routinely erase immediate frontier danger, Company unrest, direct conquest grievances or credible conflict with a subject/overlord.
- Let mood and sourced recent personal memory influence eligible voice in addition to enduring traits, without retconning personality or inventing events.
- Low independent aptitude can lead to confident error **occasionally**, not invariably; competent characters may still choose risky or self-serving interpretations due to vanity or ambition. Avoid ungrounded stochastic rotation, especially on reads, and preserve exact saved text through transactional history.
- Preserve the stable existing evidence boundary. No hidden deck order, guessed company Army inventory, invented payments, actual correspondence or untracked military preparations.
