## Eligibility and integration notes

- Situations bearing existing keys `strong-attacker`, `weak-attacker`, `weak-defender`, `strong-defender`, `company-frontier`, `company-primary`, `company-secondary`, `occupied-discontent`, `subject`, `capital`, `military-climate`, `mixed-climate`, and `commercial-disruption` must be grounded in the existing 09A projection and the current rules. Company Army inventories remain unknown.
- The personal `grievance` and `qualified` families additionally require actual owned memories and sourced relationship reasons. In particular, lines about soldiers/assistance cannot be chosen just because an intervention occurred; qualify the type of reported intervention or use a neutral rewrite.
- **Priority:** Personal direct threat, actual unrest, and sourced grievances usually outrank a generic all-Crisis Shuffle climate. Do not make every ruler produce the same general alarm. Strong public evidence can change mood; it does not guarantee a battle or victory.
- Selection should be deterministic at a meaningful **committed** transition, persisted verbatim across UI reads, save/load, snapshots, import/export and Undo/Redo. Reading/cancelling must not advance RNG or rotate lines. Keep previously saved lines unchanged in existing campaigns.
- Preserve fact guards and exact narrative-on/off mechanical equivalence. Authoring example situations are not proof of unrecorded diplomatic approaches, troop movements or actual merchant losses.
- The writing is deliberately candidate text. Some lines may be rejected or revised during creative review, especially if a metaphor sounds too modern or overstates supported evidence. **Do not claim creative approval based on passing tests.**
- After editorial approval, implement a small bounded set of personality variants in the existing data-driven outlook architecture. No 09C/09D advice, speech relocation, portraits, succession, repository cleanup, or deployment.
