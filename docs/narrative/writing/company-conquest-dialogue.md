**Triggers:** Committed `company-conquest` fact for an Indian-controlled region, never simply a failed deployment or unrest-clearing intervention.  
**Scope:** Content only. `{region}` and `{ruler}` refer to validated narrative identities; all statements must reflect the committed event.  
**Implementation:** Adapt to an offline authored content module; do not modify gameplay.

## Whole authored exchanges

Each exchange is indivisible. The record is a fictional scene resulting from the conquest, **not** proof that a mechanical diplomatic negotiation took place.

### EX-C01 — A peaceful settlement

- **Requirements:** Company conquest; surviving eligible proud/sarcastic ruler; supported Company military adviser; allowed direct fictional dialogue.
- **Tone:** Colonial self-justification and ruler's acid wit.
- **Lines:**
  1. **Company adviser:** “We trust His Highness appreciates the Company's desire for a peaceful settlement.”
  2. **Indian ruler:** “Naturally. Your affection for peace is evident from the extraordinary number of soldiers you brought to discuss it.”
- **Possible future memory:** The adviser's exact statement about peace, with speaker and audience. Later callbacks must refer to what was said, not declare it a truthful policy.

### EX-C02 — New political circumstances

- **Requirements:** Company conquest; eligible cautious ruler; supported adviser.
- **Tone:** Diplomatic restraint.
- **Lines:**
  1. **Company adviser:** “We trust His Highness recognises the new political circumstances.”
  2. **Indian ruler:** “Perfectly. I merely reserve judgment on their duration.”

### EX-C03 — Acknowledging arms

- **Requirements:** Company conquest; proud/sarcastic ruler; eligible overconfident Company military adviser.
- **Tone:** Status conflict.
- **Lines:**
  1. **Company adviser:** “His Highness must acknowledge the superiority of the Company's arms.”
  2. **Indian ruler:** “I acknowledge that you have brought a remarkable number of them.”

## Presentation and prioritisation

Choose either a suitable standalone ruler response and adviser response, **or** an eligible exchange—not both by default. Keep ordinary scenes brief and expandable. The chronicler may contribute one grounded observation but need not always deliver a joke. Save the complete chosen dialogue, expressions, templates, evidence links, speakers, statement identifiers and cooldowns at commit.

Do not force a high-pride response on a cautious ruler or assume that all defeated rulers are humiliated in the same way. If evidence or candidate variety is insufficient, use a sober factual fallback rather than fabricating specifics.

## Explicitly not approved

No additional war mechanics; no automatic deaths, appointments, palace privileges, private deals or negotiated political outcomes; no AI-generated text. This catalogue is writing content ready for **technical integration after separate authorisation**, not a mandate to rewrite rule logic.
