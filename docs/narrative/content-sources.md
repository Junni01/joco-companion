# Narrative content sources

The first playable slice uses fictional personal identities and households. Names are combined with regional labels rather than copied as the full identity of a historical ruler. Every initial biography is explicitly fictional background. No source below supports the invented relatives, personalities, campaign actions or dialogue.

## Research basis

- [Harvard South Asian history timeline](https://timelinesouthasianhistory.hsites.harvard.edu/mughal-disintegration-and-rise-regional-powers-c-1707-ce-1766-ce): regional court context, Maratha personal-name components and Mysore naming context. This supplies a starting reference, not a demographic distribution or a mandate to use historical rulers.
- [V&A on Mughal art and courts](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-arts-of-the-mughal-empire): the mixture of court traditions and regional influences. The generic court/archive backgrounds are invented; they do not attribute any specific historical court practice to a generated person.
- [British Museum Mysore coin record](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/C_1919-0406-1): an attested Krishna Raja name in the Mysore context. Generated combinations are fictional identities, not this ruler.
- [National Army Museum on the Company armies](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/armies-east-india-company): three Presidency armies and the distinction between Company forces and allied polities. Fictional secretaries and adjutants represent institutions without identifying player officeholders. Do not infer inventories or actual losses from this context.
- [Nelson-Atkins portrait of the Nawab of Arcot](https://art.nelson-atkins.org/objects/18928/): a southern court naming reference. It does not identify the fictional ruler of the board's entire Madras region.
- [British Museum Sikh coin record](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/C_1902-0608-339): a reminder that Punjab has varied political and naming traditions. The initial compact catalogue is illustrative, not an exhaustive representation.

## Content policy and limits

The small personal-name pools are provisional, research-informed fiction. Region selection chooses a naming pool, never personality or morality. Courtly names are not assertions about the ethnicity, religion or political uniformity of a board region. Expand and review these pools before treating them as a comprehensive historical catalogue. The first slice uses no historical invader identities, specific treasure catalogue or named alliance representatives; those require further research.

All dialogue is newly authored fiction. Templates in `dist/narrative-content.js` have stable IDs, trigger, speaker role, required facts, tone, expression and provenance. Chronicle summaries are composed from structured incident facts in `dist/narrative.js`; they never parse gameplay instruction strings. Names and places are escaped when rendered.

No template may establish an unreported ship loss, actual China profit, named officer death, player motive, conspiracy, promise or parentage. The initial family tie is a saved fictional sibling relationship; no parent is invented. Initial Delhi subject episodes keep unknown origins. Fate selection in this milestone retains the incumbent; natural death, weighted replacements, exiles and restoration branches remain deferred.
